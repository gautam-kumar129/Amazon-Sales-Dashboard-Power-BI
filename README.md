# 📊 Amazon Sales Dashboard | Power BI

Hi! I'm Gautam Kumar, and this is one of my Power BI projects where I worked with Amazon sales data to understand sales performance, products, customers, regions, and shipping patterns.

The main idea behind this project was simple: take raw sales data, analyze it from different angles, and present the important information through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Amazon sales data using Power BI and Excel.

The dashboard provides a clear view of:

- Overall sales and profit
- Sales trends over time
- Product performance
- Customer performance
- Regional and city-wise sales
- Category and sub-category performance
- Shipping methods and order trends

Interactive filters are used throughout the dashboard so that the data can be explored based on different countries, regions, cities, categories, months, and years.

# 🔍 Analysis & Key Insights

After exploring the dashboard, I found several useful patterns in the data.

### 💰 Overall Performance

The dashboard shows approximately **2.30M in total sales** and **286.40K in total profit**, with a profit margin of around **12.47%**.

This gives a quick picture of the overall financial performance represented in the dataset.

### 📦 Product Performance

The Product Analysis page shows that sales and profit are not evenly distributed across products.

A few products contribute significantly more to sales than others. Identifying these products can help businesses understand which products have stronger demand.

### 🌎 Regional Performance

Sales vary across different regions and cities.

The regional and city-wise visuals make it easier to identify stronger markets and compare their contribution to total sales.

### 👥 Customer Performance

The Top 10 Customers analysis shows that some customers contribute considerably more sales than others.

Understanding these high-value customers can be useful for customer retention and relationship management.

### 📅 Sales Trends

The monthly and quarterly visuals show that sales change over time.

Looking at these trends can help businesses understand periods of stronger and weaker performance and plan future sales activities accordingly.

### 🚚 Shipping Analysis

Standard Class is the most commonly used shipping mode in the analyzed data.

The shipping page also helps compare order patterns across different shipping methods and regions.

### 📊 Profitability

Looking only at sales does not give the complete picture.

The dashboard also compares sales with profit and profit margin, which helps provide a better understanding of business performance.

---

# 💡 Business Takeaways

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Focus on products that generate strong sales and profit.
- Identify and retain high-value customers.
- Compare regional performance to find stronger markets.
- Monitor monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Track profit along with sales instead of looking at revenue alone.
- Understand shipping preferences to improve operational planning.
- Investigate lower-performing products and regions for improvement opportunities.
